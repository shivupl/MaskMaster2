How Mask Master works

  1. Upload an image — this is what will be clipped by the mask.
  2. Upload a mask image (PNG or similar with transparency) — transparent areas hide your image; opaque areas show it.
  3. Use Advanced settings to rotate, scale, feather, or invert the mask.
  4. When it looks right, tap Add to canvas to place the result on your design.
