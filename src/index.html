How Mask Master works

  1. Upload an image — this is what will be clipped by the mask.
  2. Select something on your Express page (a shape, element, text, layer group, etc.) — that becomes the mask: lighter areas show more of your uploaded image.
  3. Tap Mask to rasterize the first selected item and load it into the preview.
  4. Use Advanced settings to rotate, scale, feather, or invert the mask.
  5. When it looks right, tap Add to canvas to place the result on your design.
